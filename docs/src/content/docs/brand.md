**Hora** means "hours". The Horai, the Greek goddesses of the hours, also
kept the gates of Olympus. In old towns the night watchman walked the
streets and called the hours: *"Ten o'clock, and all is well."* A status
page does the same job: it says the time and the state, in one sentence.

Hora's watcher is a **little owl** (*Athene noctua*). It dozes while all is
well, opens one eye when something is slow, and wakes only for a real
outage. The tagline: **Hora keeps the hours, so you can sleep.**

The full brand book, with every mockup, lives in
[`brand/`](https://github.com/uplg/hora/tree/main/brand) in the repository.

  the cream owl asleep. Use it as an avatar, an app icon or beside the name.
- **The wordmark**: the mark, then **Hora** in Borel Display, set as
  outlines. Ink on light grounds, paper on dark ones, or on petrol.
- **The favicon** is redrawn on a 16-pixel grid: tile, silhouette, tufts and
  two shut eyes.

## The owl and the states

The owl's pose is the page's overall state. It never carries the state on
its own: a sentence always says it, and a shape repeats it.

| | State | Pose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <img src="/hora/brand/owl-up.svg" alt="" width="56" height="56" /> | Up | Asleep, both eyes shut |
| <img src="/hora/brand/owl-degraded.svg" alt="" width="56" height="56" /> | Slow | One eye open, one tuft up |
| <img src="/hora/brand/owl-down.svg" alt="" width="56" height="56" /> | Down | Awake, both eyes open, tufts up |
| <img src="/hora/brand/owl-maint.svg" alt="" width="56" height="56" /> | Maintenance | Asleep, tufts folded: a planned nap |
| <img src="/hora/brand/owl-none.svg" alt="" width="56" height="56" /> | No data | Two dashes, no blush |

Rules for the owl:

1. **Never sad, never scared.** No mouth, no tears. "Down" is awake and
   attentive.
2. **It moves only in answer to something**: once, when the state changes.
   No idle animation.
3. **Never the only carrier of the state.** Always beside the sentence that
   says it.
4. **One per page**, small, at the top or in an empty state. Never in rows,
   tables or charts.

Light value first, dark second. Every text pair meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) and
every state graphic meets 3:1 on the surfaces it is drawn on; the ratios are in the brand book.

Each state has a **colour, a shape and a word**: a filled disc for up, a
half disc for slow, a diamond with "!" for down, a clock for maintenance, a
dashed ring for no data. The shape survives greyscale and colour
blindness; the word is always there.

## Type

- **Fraunces**: titles and the sentence you read first.
- **Cal Sans**: everything else.
- **Borel Display**: the name, beside the mark, and nowhere else.
- **Mona Sans Mono**: configuration, commands and captured answers.

All four are under the SIL Open Font License.

## Voice

Calm, plain and factual, a little warm. Say the time, the state and what
people will notice. No exclamation marks, no blame, no jargon on public
pages, and no em-dashes.

| Instead of | Hora says |
| --- | --- |
| All Systems Operational | Everything is running. |
| Major outage on payments-api | Card payments are down since 14:02. |
| Vantage disagreement 1/3 | Our Paris checkpoint can't reach it; the others can. Not an outage. |
| Resolved | Up again at 19:53. Down for 41 min. |

## Using the brand

You may use the mark and the name to link to Hora, to say that you use it,
or in an article about it. Keep the mark as drawn: do not recolour the owl,
add a mouth or an expression, or stretch it. Please do not use it as the
logo of another product.
